# Phase 7 — Clickable Prototype: Close-out (2026-10-23)

> ## ⚠ Read first
> 1. **Phase 7 is closed as PARTIALLY COMPLETE.** It is not fully complete, and it is **not approved**.
> 2. **Policy-dependent prototype branches are demonstration alternatives, not approved business rules.** They follow `docs/policy-defaults.md`. The only explicitly approved rule is "Accepted keeps the contact reservation". Clicking through a branch does not approve the policy it shows.
> 3. **The Owner export "Preparing → Success" click-to-advance is a prototype simplification.** The real export is synchronous and is generated by the server. The user never advances it by clicking.
> 4. **Prototype completeness is separate from screen-design completeness.** The Phase 6 screens are approved, and their status is in `docs/phase6/screen-register.md`. This file covers only how much of that design is clickable.
> 5. **Syntax checks and dry runs are not wiring.** "Verified" below means the script ran in Figma and its read-back matched.

## Why it stopped
Twice, a model safeguard or safety classifier ended an operator session. The second stop came during `owner/P41-05`. Message:
"Interrupted: the response that made this tool call was stopped by a safety classifier while the call was running; it may have partially or fully completed."
By user instruction, prototype wiring stopped there. There will be no further recovery sessions and no re-runs, and no Figma writes, labels included, were made during this close-out.
