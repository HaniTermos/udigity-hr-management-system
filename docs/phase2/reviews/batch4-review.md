## Verdict

**Ready after listed functional fixes** (6 required fixes, all Major, no Blocker).

What checks out and needs no change:
- Accepted opportunity is kept apart from closed deal. Closed = accepted + signed + paid. Closed deal is kept apart from completed project.
- E1/E2/E3: the deal record is created in the same save, and deferring the Developer assignment is an explicit, derived condition.
- SM-MEETING-001 treats "rescheduled" as an event (R-13), and Missed is Proposed. No finalized meeting time is required at acceptance.
- DEC-006, DEC-017, DEC-018 and F2 all stay unselected, and no attachments are required. F3 is respected: closure stays committed when provisioning fails.
- Project status and task status are separate and non-linear, with no forced pass through Waiting or Blocked. DEC-019 statuses are Proposed.
- Session revocation is kept apart from account deactivation. Deactivation revokes sessions. Nothing is reassigned automatically.
- The Owner never reads a password. Password reset, Owner-triggered assistance, 2FA enrollment and 2FA recovery are all present.
- Reports and exports are Owner only, use filters, have a no-data state and an export-failure path, and exports are audited. The G1 pairs are separate metrics.
- Mermaid structure (script check): all 16 flowcharts have every referenced node defined, every decision exit labelled, and 12–23 nodes. The 6 state diagrams have no `note`, `classDef` or `style`, and all states are declared.

## Minor (not required)

- **Stale baseline and BCRs.** All three files say "Baseline SB-1". BCR-01, BCR-04, BCR-05 and BCR-08 are now SB-2 rulings R-13, R-04, R-05 and R-09, so cite those rulings instead of open requests. BCR-02 and BCR-07 are covered by R-06 and Batch 2 Table RT.
- **2FA recovery revocation scope.** WF-AUTH-001 L4 says 2FA recovery revokes "all of that employee's sessions". WF-AUTH-002 E2 and SM-SESSION-001 say "other sessions". Pick one wording.
- **Mermaid #5 Owner path.** The Owner reaches only "Owner assigns". The Owner's Confirmed right to update status and schedule (spec step 7, Board "Developers and Owner view and update") is not drawn.
- **Mermaid #2 attending Call Center.** The outcome node says "Developer or Owner records outcome" and leaves out the attending Call Center agent named in the spec.
- **Mermaid #7 end label.** `ended` reads "Session Ended: abandoned" but is reached on attempt threshold exceeded. Label it with the matching end reason.
- **Not proceeding leaves meetings open.** SM-COMMERCIAL-001 "Mark not proceeding" cancels pending meeting reminders but leaves the meetings Scheduled. State what happens to those meetings (cancel them, or leave it as an open question).
- **Developer Alt 1 handoff updates.** SM-COMMERCIAL-001 "Handoff context updated" lists "Developer (Alt 1)". Batch 2 VT-5 row 1 gives the Developer under Alt 1 only "read-only contact and notes, meetings, flag correction", and gives hand-off updates to Call Center. Align, or raise a request to Batch 2.
- **Confirmed tag on M2.** WF-EMP-001 M2 says "(Confirmed instruction: no automatic rule invented)". That is not a Tier-1 rule. Tag it Proposed (consistent with R-02).
- **DEC-006 alternative labels.** The Mermaid #4 labels "Alt A / Alt B" should carry "(drafted from ART-01 §12)", as Batch 2 does for alternatives that are not on Board 01.
- **Correction requests on deactivation.** WF-EMP-001 M3 / Mermaid #12 list open correction requests but give them no hand-off (for example `[[WF-BIZ-002]]`).
- **Logout event naming.** Logout from a pending state uses `session.ended (reason)`, while logout from Active uses `session.logged_out`. Consider one event per end reason.
